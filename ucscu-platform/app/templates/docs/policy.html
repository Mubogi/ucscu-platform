UCSCU HR & conduct policy

Summary of rules that govern attendance, visitors, reporting and conduct on this platform

1. Attendance

Office staff must check in and out on the office LAN. The system rejects check-ins from outside the premises network. Field officers and board members file an exemption check-in stating their location and reason. Arrivals after 08:30 require a lateness reason in the check-in form.

2. Leave and permission

Leave, absence and late-arrival notifications are requested through this platform and approved by line management (staff/board/admin roles). Approved leave appears on the shared calendar.

3. Visitors

All visitors must be registered at reception, issued a numbered tag, and checked out when they leave. Tags are recycled as visitors leave. Items brought in are recorded.

4. Communication hierarchy

Hub spaces are scoped: Everyone, UCSCU staff & board, Board & admin, and SACCO leaders. Post only where you have access — confidential material belongs in restricted spaces.

5. Confidential reporting (whistleblowing)

Reports of fraud, corruption, harassment or misuse of funds may be filed anonymously. The system never stores the reporter's identity. Reports go to the oversight committee; status can be checked with the issued token. Retaliation against a whist-leblower is a disciplinary offence.

6. Meeting registers and minutes

Attendance registers are opened per meeting; minutes use approved templates (General, Board, AGM, Attendance register). Minutes marked as Policy must be approved by the Board before they take effect.
